AI-Based Automated Floor Plan Analysis and Building-Regulation Compliance Checking for Sri Lankan Local Government Authorities

Intelligent plan review, rule-checking, and design advisory system for Sri Lankan building approval workflows
This component supports early-stage building-plan review by combining AI-based blueprint analysis, engineering validation, climate-aware recommendations, and regulatory compliance assessment for local authority decision-making.

Problem Addressed

Manual building-plan review is time-consuming, inconsistent, and vulnerable to oversight in screening and preliminary compliance checks.

  • Slow approval workflows for local authorities
  • Difficulty in reviewing spatial feasibility and room adequacy
  • Limited standardization in preliminary compliance checks
  • Need for climate- and safety-aware plan assessment

Proposed Solution

An AI-assisted digital review assistant that interprets uploaded floor plans and evaluates them against structural, occupancy, and engineering logic before senior human review.

  • Computer vision-based blueprint interpretation
  • Rule-based validation of density and adequacy
  • Climate-sensitive material recommendations
  • Explainable review feedback for authorities

1. System Overview

The platform accepts a floor plan image and associated building profile, analyzes the spatial arrangement, and then evaluates the design against engineering constraints and regulatory-oriented heuristics. It provides review warnings, suggested improvements, and material recommendations.

3. Core Compliance Logic

Compliance Checks Included

  • Space adequacy by building type
  • Occupancy-to-bathroom ratio checks
  • Area per occupant validation
  • Educational classroom capacity checks
  • Healthcare bed-density and room adequacy
  • Commercial employee density checks
  • Structural safety and engineering veto rules
  • Climate-compatibility and material suitability review

Typical Output

  • Validation score
  • Severity: low / medium / high
  • Warnings and recommendations
  • Structural and occupancy issues flagged
  • Material suitability suggestions
  • Preliminary engineering review summary

4. Sri Lankan Context

The platform is aligned with Sri Lankan planning conditions through climate-aware decision support, engineering validation logic, and references to SLS- and BS-related construction standards. It is designed to support local-government review environments where climate, structure, and practical feasibility matter.

  • Adaptation to coastal, wet, dry, and highland conditions
  • Material suitability based on corrosion, humidity, and environmental exposure
  • Engineering logic aimed at safe preliminary screening
  • Conceptual support for local authority building review workflows

5. Benefits to Local Government Authorities

  • Reduces review time in initial building-plan screening
  • Standardizes plan assessment across cases and reviewers
  • Identifies likely non-compliance before manual review
  • Improves transparency with explainable warnings and recommendations
  • Supports early decisions on project suitability and engineering risk

6. Key Limitations

This is a strong research and decision-support prototype rather than a fully legal municipal compliance engine. For actual deployment, the system needs formal mapping to official Sri Lankan building regulations, municipal bylaws, and approved approval checklists.

  • Preliminary review, not final legal compliance certification
  • Dependent on quality of uploaded plans and building metadata
  • Requires formal policy mapping for local authority enforcement
  • Requires human expert verification for construction approval decisions

7. Final Summary

In summary, the component is an AI-assisted automated floor-plan analysis and building-regulation compliance review platform designed to support Sri Lankan local government authorities. It combines vision-based plan interpretation, rule-driven engineering checks, climate-aware material reasoning, and explainable review outputs to help speed up and improve early approval screening.
