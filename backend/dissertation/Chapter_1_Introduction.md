# Chapter 1: Introduction

## 1.1 Background
The construction industry is rapidly evolving with the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Building Information Modeling (BIM). Intelligent systems are now capable of automating complex architectural and engineering tasks, from spatial layout generation to sustainable material specification. GreenConstructAI represents a novel approach to streamlining these early-stage design processes.

## 1.2 Problem Statement
Despite advancements in generative design, there remains a disconnect between architectural layout generation, structural engineering constraints, and sustainable material specification. Existing solutions often treat these as isolated steps, leading to suboptimal space utilization, structural incompatibilities, or poor sustainability outcomes.

## 1.3 Research Gap
Current intelligent design systems lack a unified, multi-criteria optimization pipeline that simultaneously evaluates layouts using machine learning (ML) aesthetics, engineering rule-based constraints, and environmental sustainability indices. 

## 1.4 Aim
This research aims to develop and validate a comprehensive, AI-driven architectural design pipeline (GreenConstructAI) that automates blueprint generation, intelligent furniture placement, and sustainable material recommendation while scoring layouts using a hybrid evaluation system.

## 1.5 Objectives
1. To develop a generative blueprint engine capable of creating valid floor plans across multiple building types (Residential, Hotel, Hospital, Office).
2. To implement an intelligent furniture layout engine that optimizes space utilization.
3. To construct a sustainable material recommendation system sensitive to local climate zones.
4. To formulate a Hybrid Scoring mechanism combining ML-based design evaluation and deterministic engineering rules.
5. To validate the pipeline using a large-scale dataset of generated scenarios.

## 1.6 Scope
The scope of this research encompasses the backend generation and evaluation of 2D floor plans, spatial programs, furniture coordinates, and material selections. It is bounded to Sri Lankan climate zones and four distinct building typologies.

## 1.7 Research Questions
1. How can procedural algorithms and machine learning be combined to automate holistic building design?
2. What is the impact of varying budget tiers and climate zones on the sustainability and functional coverage of generated layouts?
3. How does the proposed Hybrid Score compare against independent ML or engineering evaluations in assessing layout viability?
