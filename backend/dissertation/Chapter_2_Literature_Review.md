# Chapter 2: Literature Review

## 2.1 AI in Construction
Artificial intelligence has seen accelerated adoption in construction, particularly in generative design, predictive maintenance, and project scheduling.

## 2.2 Sustainable Material Recommendation
Selecting building materials based on environmental impact and embodied energy is critical for green building design. Decision support systems using multi-criteria decision making (MCDM) help balance cost, structural integrity, and sustainability.

## 2.3 BIM
Building Information Modeling provides a digital representation of physical and functional characteristics of places. Integrating AI directly into BIM workflows allows for rapid prototyping and automated compliance checking.

## 2.4 Machine Learning
Machine learning models, particularly deep neural networks, have shown promise in evaluating the aesthetic and functional quality of architectural layouts based on historical data.

## 2.5 Multi-Criteria Decision Making
MCDM techniques allow for the evaluation of conflicting criteria (e.g., budget vs. sustainability) during the material selection process.

## 2.6 Automated Floor Planning
Procedural generation and generative adversarial networks (GANs) are frequently utilized to automate the generation of floor plans given boundary constraints and spatial program requirements.

## 2.7 Intelligent Furniture Placement
Optimizing interior spaces requires algorithms that can navigate non-convex geometries and place items according to functional clearance zones and ergonomic principles.

## 2.8 Research Gap Summary
The literature reveals a clear need for systems that integrate these disparate technologies into a cohesive, end-to-end pipeline. Most existing tools focus exclusively on either material science, structural analysis, or layout generation, without bridging the gap through a unified evaluation metric like the proposed Hybrid Score.
